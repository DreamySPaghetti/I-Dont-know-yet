# Inkwell

A distraction-free Markdown writing app for Android.

- Markdown editor with live styling (headings, bold, italic, lists, quotes)
- Library of groups and sheets
- Focus mode, word goals, word/character counts, reading time
- Export to Markdown, plain text or PDF, or share
- Everything is saved on your phone only

## Install on your phone

1. On your Android phone, open https://github.com/DreamySPaghetti/I-Dont-know-yet/releases/latest
2. Tap **Inkwell.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to "install unknown apps".
4. Tap **Install**, then open Inkwell.

Every push to `main` rebuilds the app automatically (see the Actions tab).
